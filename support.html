Support

HopOver: Animal Jump

Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Email dunghm.work@gmail.com. Please include your device model, OS version and what happened. We usually reply within a few days.

How to play

Animals run along four lanes. Tap a lane to make its front animal jump over the obstacle. Jump at the right moment for a Perfect and keep a combo going for more points and coins. Each miss costs a heart; the round ends when the hearts run out.

Frequently asked questions

I bought something but did not get it

Open Shop > Gems and tap RESTORE. This gives back purchases that are kept forever, such as No ads. If gems are missing after a purchase, email us with the date and the receipt from the App Store or Google Play.

I reinstalled the app and lost my progress

Progress (scores, coins, gems, unlocked maps, animals and skins) is saved only on your device, so deleting the app deletes it too. Purchases kept forever can be restored with RESTORE; gems that were already spent cannot.

How do I remove ads?

Buy No ads in Shop > Gems. It removes the ads between rounds. Ads you choose to watch for a reward (revive, double coins, opening a chest early) stay available.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where the law requires it, Settings has a PRIVACY button that reopens the consent form. On iPhone and iPad you can also change tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. See the Privacy Policy for details.

The game is too hard or too easy

Pick Easy, Normal or Hard in Settings. Each difficulty keeps its own top scores.
