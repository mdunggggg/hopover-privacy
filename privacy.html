Privacy Policy

HopOver: Animal Jump · Effective November 17, 2026

HopOver: Animal Jump ("the app") is a mobile game made by Manh Dung Hoang ("imdungs", "we"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why. If you have questions, email dunghm.work@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (scores, coins, gems, unlocked items, settings, daily rewards and missions) is saved only on your device. We do not run our own servers and do not receive this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent first (Google's consent message), and you can choose non-personalized ads. You can also reset or limit the advertising identifier in your device settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Buying "No ads" removes the ads shown between rounds. Ads you choose to watch for a reward (for example to revive) stay available.

Analytics

The app may use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played (for example rounds started, scores, purchases and settings changes) so we can improve it. This uses an app instance identifier and device information, not your name or contact details. See the Firebase privacy information.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. To confirm and restore purchases, the app may use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and the purchase receipt from the store. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

What we do not collect

  • No account, name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • No precise location, photos, camera or microphone.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices and rights

You can change ad consent in the app's settings where offered, limit ad tracking in your device settings, and delete all local data by uninstalling the app. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA) you may have rights to access or delete data held by the services above; contact us and we will help with requests.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
